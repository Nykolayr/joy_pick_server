Privacy Policy

Joy Pick — Last updated: January 2025

This Privacy Policy explains how Joy Pick ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our application and services (the "Service"). By using the Service, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide and information that is generated when you use the Service.

  • Account and profile: email address, display name, first and last name, phone number (if provided), profile photo, and similar identifiers you give us when registering or editing your profile.
  • Authentication: If you sign in with Google, Apple, or another provider, we receive identifiers (e.g. email, name) from that provider as permitted by their policies.
  • Location: If you allow the app to use your location, we may collect latitude and longitude to show nearby requests, partners, or recycling stations. You can disable location in your device settings.
  • Content you create: Photos and other content you upload (e.g. for requests, profile), messages you send in chats, and data you enter in forms.
  • Volunteer and partner activity: Participation in requests, Joycoins balance and history, redemptions at partners (including one-time QR tokens and transaction details), and related usage data.
  • Payment and payout data: When you make donations or receive payouts, we and our payment provider (e.g. Stripe) process data necessary for the transaction (e.g. account identifiers, transaction amounts). We do not store full card numbers; payment details are handled by the payment provider.
  • Device and app use: Device type, operating system, push notification tokens (e.g. FCM), and general usage of the app (e.g. to fix errors and improve the Service).

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the Service (accounts, requests, Joycoins, partner redemptions, chats, payments);
  • Authenticate you and manage your account;
  • Send you push notifications (e.g. about requests, messages, reminders) if you have enabled them;
  • Process donations and payouts and prevent fraud;
  • Respond to support requests and enforce our Terms of Service;
  • Comply with legal obligations and protect our rights and safety;
  • Analyze usage in an aggregated, non-personally-identifying way to improve the Service.

3. Sharing of Information

We may share your information in the following cases:

  • Partners: When you redeem Joycoins at a partner (e.g. via QR), the partner's system receives data needed for the transaction (e.g. user identifier, amount, branch). Partner use of that data is governed by the partner's own policies.
  • Service providers: We use third-party services for hosting, authentication (e.g. Firebase), payments (e.g. Stripe), push notifications (e.g. FCM), and email. They process data on our behalf under agreements that require them to protect your data.
  • Other users: Your profile information (e.g. name, photo) and content you post (e.g. in chats, on requests) may be visible to other users or partners as required for the feature.
  • Legal: We may disclose information if required by law, court order, or to protect our or others' rights, safety, or property.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties for their marketing.

4. Data Retention

We keep your data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Service, resolve disputes, and comply with legal obligations. After account deletion, we may retain some data in backup or anonymized form where permitted by law. Transaction and redemption records may be kept for accounting and legal purposes.

5. Security

We use technical and organizational measures (e.g. encryption, access controls, secure connections) to protect your data. No system is completely secure; you are responsible for keeping your password and device secure.

6. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access and receive a copy of the personal data we hold about you;
  • Correct inaccurate data;
  • Request deletion of your data (subject to legal retention needs);
  • Object to or restrict certain processing;
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent (e.g. push notifications);
  • Lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

You can update your profile and, in the app, manage notification settings. For other requests, contact us at rail.demarco@gmail.com.

7. Children

The Service is not directed at children under 13 (or the applicable minimum age). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe we have collected such data, please contact us so we can delete it.

8. International Transfers

Your data may be processed in countries other than your own. We ensure appropriate safeguards (e.g. standard contractual clauses or other approved mechanisms) where required by law.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version on this page and update the "Last updated" date. Continued use of the Service after changes means you accept the updated policy. We encourage you to review it periodically.

10. Contact

For privacy-related questions or to exercise your rights, contact us at rail.demarco@gmail.com.
